PCMT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

Personal Content Moderation Tool (PCMT) helps Instagram creators automatically moderate comments on their own content based on policies they define. This privacy policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

When you connect your Instagram account to PCMT, we collect:

  • Instagram account information: Your Instagram user ID and username, required to identify which account's comments to moderate.
  • Comment data: When someone comments on your posts, Meta sends us a webhook notification containing the comment ID, the commenter's username, and the comment text. We use this to apply your moderation policy.
  • Moderation decisions: We log which comments were flagged, hidden, or deleted, and why, so you can review our actions.

How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver comment moderation according to the policy you configure
  • To provide you with a log of moderation actions for review
  • To improve the accuracy of our moderation engine

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with third parties except as required to operate the service (hosting infrastructure).

Data Storage

Comment data and moderation logs are stored securely on our servers. We retain moderation logs for 90 days, after which they are automatically deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Disconnect your Instagram account at any time (this stops all data collection immediately)
  • Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data

To exercise these rights, email developer@craftpath.online.

Data Deletion

If you disconnect PCMT from your Instagram account, we stop collecting new data immediately. To request deletion of historical data, email developer@craftpath.online and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

PCMT is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or through the service.
