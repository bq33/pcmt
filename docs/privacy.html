Privacy Policy

Personal Content Moderation Tool (PCMT)

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Overview

PCMT is an open-source, human-reviewed, owner-configurable comment moderation policy for creators. It is designed to run in audit-only mode by default: it reviews comments and replies on content you own and proposes moderation actions, but takes no action on its own.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not run any background API, analytics beacon, or telemetry in the PCMT policy files, tools, or demo pages.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your social media credentials.
  • We do not build profiles of commenters or visitors.

Information You Provide

If you configure PCMT, you may provide:

  • Owner preferences, such as moderation presets (balanced, aggressive, maximum) and optional private term blocklists you define yourself.
  • Comment text you choose to evaluate with the offline scoring utilities (for example, tools/score_spam.py), which run locally and send nothing over the network.

Owner-specific configuration is intended to stay private to you. Do not publish handles, raw doxxing content, or private comments in public issues or pull requests.

How Information Is Used

Any information you provide is used only to operate PCMT for your own accounts: auditing comments, proposing dispositions (such as hiding), and improving your local moderation policy. Rule matches are never blanket permissions to take account actions; deletion always requires per-comment approval by you.

Third-Party Services

PCMT itself includes no direct integration with Meta or any other platform. If you connect PCMT output to Instagram, Threads, or another platform, that platform’s own privacy policy governs data it collects. PCMT sends nothing to third parties on your behalf.

Your Choices

  • You may run PCMT entirely offline and in audit-only mode, in which case nothing leaves your machine.
  • You may delete your local configuration at any time.

Data Retention

PCMT retains no data of its own. Anything stored by your deployment (for example, logs) is controlled by you and can be deleted by you.

Children’s Privacy

PCMT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as PCMT evolves. The current version is always available at docs/privacy.html in the repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please open an issue at github.com/bq33/pcmt.
