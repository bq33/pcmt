PCMT Terms of Service

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. The Service

Personal Content Moderation Tool (PCMT) is a comment moderation service for Instagram creators. It connects to your Instagram professional account via Meta's official API and automatically evaluates comments on your content against moderation policies you define (blocked terms, patterns, and user blocklists). Depending on the operating mode you select, PCMT will flag, hide, or delete matching comments, and log every decision for your review.

2. Your Account and Responsibilities

  • You must own or be authorized to manage the Instagram account you connect.
  • You define your own moderation policy. PCMT applies it mechanically; you are responsible for the rules you set and for reviewing the moderation log.
  • You may disconnect your Instagram account at any time, which immediately stops all moderation activity.

3. Operating Modes

PCMT offers three modes: audit only (flags matches and logs them, takes no action), active hide (hides matching comments), and active delete (deletes matching comments). Audit-only mode is the default. Switching to an active mode is your choice and your responsibility.
