# PCMT Production Setup Guide (Path 1: SaaS)

This guide covers taking PCMT from a development app to a production service that any creator can use.

## Step 1: Complete Basic App Settings

In your Meta app dashboard (Settings > Basic):

1. Privacy Policy URL: Add a link to your privacy policy (required for Live mode)
2. App Icon: Upload a 1024x1024 icon
3. Category: Select a category (e.g., Business, Utilities)
4. Terms of Service URL: Recommended

## Step 2: Business Verification

1. Go to your Meta Business Settings (business.facebook.com)
2. Navigate to Security Center > Business Verification
3. You'll need business legal name, address, phone, documents, and domain verification

This typically takes 1-3 business days.

## Step 3: Tech Provider Status

1. In your app dashboard, go to the permission you want
2. Click Actions > Add to App Review
3. Complete Tech Provider enrollment (requires business verification)

Note: This decision cannot be reversed.

## Step 4: Prepare App Review Submission

For instagram_business_manage_comments:
- Use case description: Explain PCMT automatically moderates Instagram comments based on user-defined policies
- Screencast: Show policy config, test comment, detection/handling, moderation log review
- Privacy policy must be accessible

## Step 5: Submit for Review

1. Complete all Data Usage questionnaires
2. Upload screencasts
3. Submit — typical review time 3-5 business days

## Step 6: Go Live

Once approved, toggle to Live mode. Creators connect via OAuth with no Meta dashboard work.
